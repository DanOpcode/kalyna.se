About Kalyna

“Kalyna” is the Ukrainian word “Калина” written in Latin letters. It is a plant known in English as guelder rose. It is a traditional Ukrainian symbol whose berries represent the homeland. In Ukraine, the guelder rose appears in songs, art, embroidery and poetry. One now well-known song is “Oh, the Red Viburnum in the Meadow”, written in 1914. Its original title is “Ой у лузі червона калина”, and it is still sung today.
