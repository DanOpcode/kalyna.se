## About Kalyna

“Kalyna” is the Ukrainian word “Калина” written in Latin letters. It is a plant known in English as [guelder rose](<https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skogsolvon>). It is a traditional Ukrainian symbol whose berries represent the homeland. In Ukraine, the guelder rose appears in songs, art, embroidery and poetry. One now well-known song is “[Oh, the Red Viburnum in the Meadow](<https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ett_r%C3%B6tt_olvon_vid_%C3%A4ngen>)”, written in 1914. Its original title is “Ой у лузі червона калина”, and it is [still sung today](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EV_vT0Vud5Q>).
